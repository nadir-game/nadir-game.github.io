-- an ASCII original. --












Arrow Keys / WASD: move   ENTER: select   or tap
























Tap or drag on the map to paint with the lit tool: # wall, . floor (BRUSH sets its size), > stairs, @ your start, ! potion, $ gold, ( key or lockpick, + door, L locked door (shown as L here, as + in play; each lock needs a ( you can reach first), x erase, other letters place mobs. The outer ring stays rock. On the last floor the stairs end the run.
SETUP rolls random mobs and items every run (s comes as a pack of 4). TEST plays this floor, PLAY the whole dungeon, SHARE sends it to the community.






COMMUNITY LEVELS










OFFICIAL levels ship with the game. COMMUNITY levels are shared by players once a moderator approves them. Send yours with SHARE (here under MINE, or in the DEPTH EDITOR). Reports and bad levels are removed by moderators. CODE needs no app or account: paste a friend's level code to play it, or copy yours to send it.
